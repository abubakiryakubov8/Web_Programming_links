01

Initial Audit

BEFORE

65 / 100

Lighthouse identified accessibility issues that needed to be corrected.

TWO ISSUES

Language & Image

The page needed a document language declaration and alternative text for an image.

Lighthouse results before making the accessibility fixes.

02

Problems Identified

Missing Language Attribute

The HTML document did not specify its primary language. Adding lang="en" allows browsers and assistive technologies to correctly identify the language of the page.

Missing Image Alternative Text

An image did not have an alt attribute. Alternative text provides a description of the image for users who rely on screen readers or when the image cannot be displayed.

03

Changes Made

BEFORE 
<html>

...

<img
    src="image.jpg"
>
AFTER 
<html
    lang="en"
>

...

<img
    src="image.jpg"
    alt="Description of the image"
>
04

Why These Changes Matter

lang="en"

Helps screen readers pronounce and interpret the content using the correct language rules.

alt Text

Gives users who cannot see the image a textual description of its important information.

05

Final Audit

PASSED

100 / 100

After adding lang="en" to the HTML element and providing appropriate alt text for the image, the accessibility audit achieved a perfect score.

Lighthouse results after the accessibility improvements.

Two small HTML improvements increased the Lighthouse accessibility score from 65 to 100.
